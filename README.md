# Typerpunk

**Typerpunk** is a fast-paced, cyberpunk-themed arcade typing game that tests your speed, focus, and reflexes. Battle against endless waves of cyber-drones, unleash tactical energy beams from your visor, and climb the ranks to achieve the ultimate high score!

## Key Features

* **Dynamic Difficulty:** Enemy speed increases progressively as your score climbs, keeping the intensity high.
* **Adaptive Obstacles:** Cyber-drones automatically resize their chassis to perfectly frame the word currently displayed.
* **Tactical Combat:** Annihilate targets with a signature laser strike triggered immediately upon completing a word.
* **Real-time Analytics:** Monitor your WPM (*Words Per Minute*) and system status on a sleek, futuristic HUD.
* **Infinite Word Supply:** Integrated API support ensures an endless, fresh stream of words for every session.
* **Persistent Records:** Track your legacy with a browser-based `localStorage` high score system.

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Credits

* **HTML5 Canvas API:** High-performance rendering for smooth 60 FPS gameplay.
* **JavaScript (ES6+):** Robust game engine logic with asynchronous data handling.
* **CSS3:** Minimalist, neon-infused UI design.
* **Word Data:** Powered by [Random Words API](https://random-words-api.kushcreates.com/api).
